Our Services

General repairs

General repairs involve fixing and restoring various elements within a home or building, including doors, windows, cabinets, and more. This work addresses issues such as loose hinges, broken locks, damaged frames, and general wear and tear.

Electrical

Electrical work involves the installation, repair and maintenance of electrical systems to ensure safe and efficient operation. This includes wiring, lighting, circuit breaker panels, outlets, and other components that power living spaces. Safety rules and provision of reliable electrical solutions.

Plumbing

Plumbing work involves the installation, repair, and maintenance of residential piping systems for water. This includes tasks such as setting up pipes, fixtures, and appliances like sinks, toilets, and water heaters, as well as diagnosing and resolving issues like leaks or clogs.
