Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how Kapustafix (“we”, “us”) uses the personal information you give us through this website. We are responsible for your data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are and how to contact us

Kapustafix — a mobile handyman, joinery, decking and flooring service covering London and surrounding areas.
Email: kapustafix@gmail.com
Phone: 07448 219217

What information we collect

When you use our quote form, or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we collect:

  • your name and phone number;
  • your email address (optional);
  • the type of work you need and the details you write in your message, including any photos you send us on WhatsApp.

We do not ask for payment details through this website.

How and why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote. Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.
  • To carry out the work and keep basic records if you become a customer (contract, and legal obligations such as accounting).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We only use trusted service providers to run the website and receive your enquiries:

  • Form delivery service (FormSubmit) — passes the details you submit in the quote form to our email inbox.
  • Email provider — where we receive and store enquiries.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — only if you choose to send your request or photos to us via WhatsApp. Its own privacy policy applies to messages you send.
  • Website hosting and content providers — this site loads fonts and icons from third parties (for example Google Fonts and Cloudflare’s cdnjs), which means your IP address is visible to them when you open a page.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards required by UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and provide a quote, and normally delete them within 12 months if we do not work together. If we do work together, we keep the records needed for the job, guarantees and accounting for as long as the law requires.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • ask us to restrict or stop using it, or object to how we use it;
  • ask for your data in a portable format, where it applies.

To use any of these rights, email or call us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
